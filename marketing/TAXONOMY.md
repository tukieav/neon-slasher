## Rationale

Neon Slasher is real-time arena combat, so **Action** is the primary category.
Its repeated waves of specialist robots, escalating hazards, and survival score
run support Survival and Horde Survival discovery. Its gameplay and delivery are
procedural 2D, include touch controls, robot enemies, destruction feedback, and
close-range fighting; those are exactly the listed tag routes.
